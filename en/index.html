Frequently asked questions

How do I cancel my subscription?
From your iPhone's Settings → tap your name at the top → Subscriptions → Chapters → Cancel subscription. It isn't cancelled from inside the app because Apple manages it.

How do I delete my account?
Inside the app: Profile → Delete account. This permanently deletes your chapters, moments and photos. It cannot be undone, and it does not cancel your subscription on its own.

I changed phones — do I lose my memories?
No. Sign in with the same account and your content is all there.

Who can see my moments?
Only you. Photos are kept in private storage and there is no feature for sharing them publicly.
