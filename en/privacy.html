Privacy policy

Last updated: October 14, 2026 · Español

Chapters is an app for keeping your own memories. This policy explains exactly what data is stored, where, and what is done with it.

The short version: no advertising, no analytics, no cross-app tracking, and your data is never sold or shared with third parties for commercial purposes. It is also never used to train artificial intelligence models. The only thing stored is what Chapters needs in order to show you your own content.

Who is responsible for your data

Chapters is developed and operated by Agustín Castino, individually. For any privacy question: the.chapters.app@gmail.com.

What data is collected

Your account. When you sign in with Google or with Apple, we receive your email address and your name, and an internal user identifier is created. Your password is never received or stored: authentication is handled entirely by the provider you choose.

Your profile. The name you choose to display inside the app, which you can edit or leave empty.

The content you create. Chapters (name, type, icon and color), moments (title, note, date, icon and color), and the photos you attach.

Location, only if you add it. When creating a moment you can save where it happened, either by using your current location or by picking a point on the map. In that case the coordinates and the name of the place are stored, attached to that moment. The only permission requested is "while using the app": your location is never recorded in the background, and no history of your movements is ever built. If you don't add a location to a moment, none is stored.

What data is NOT collected

  • Payment details. Subscriptions are processed by Apple; Chapters never sees your card or your billing information.
  • Advertising or tracking identifiers. The app does not track your activity across other apps or websites.
  • Usage analytics, heatmaps, or session recordings.
  • Your contacts, your calendar, your microphone.

What it is used for

One purpose only: showing you your content on your devices, and keeping your account working. Nothing else.

Where it is stored

On servers operated by Supabase, located in the United States (region us-west-2), which provides the database and the file storage.

Your photos are kept in a private bucket: they are not reachable through any public URL. Each time the app needs to show you a photo, it generates a signed, temporary link that expires within an hour. Access to your rows in the database is restricted to your own account through row-level policies.

Third parties involved

  • Supabase — infrastructure: database, photo storage and authentication. (their policy)
  • Google — only if you choose to sign in with Google. (their policy)
  • Apple — app distribution, subscription processing, and sign-in if you choose it. (their policy)

No other third party has access to your data.

Notifications

Chapters' notifications are scheduled and shown on your own device. Nothing is sent to a server to generate them, and no push notification token is stored.

Your rights over your data

  • Edit. You can change or delete any chapter, moment or photo at any time, from inside the app.
  • Delete your account. From Profile → Delete account. This permanently deletes your profile, your chapters, your moments and all of your photos from storage. It cannot be undone and there is no way to recover it afterwards.
  • Request a copy. Write to the contact address and we will send you a copy of your data.

How long it is kept

As long as your account exists. When you delete it, the data is deleted at that moment — it does not sit in a retention queue.

Children

Chapters is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from anyone that age. If you believe a child under 13 created an account, write to us and we will delete it.

Changes to this policy

If it changes, the date in the header is updated. If a change significantly affects how your data is handled, you will be notified inside the app before it takes effect.
